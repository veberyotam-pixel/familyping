Add FamilyPing to your Home Screen

iPhone will only let the app ring you once it lives on your Home Screen.

  1. Tap the Share button at the bottom of Safari ⬆️
  2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen
  3. Tap Add, then open FamilyPing from your Home Screen
📣

Get FamilyPing for Android

The Android app rings even when your phone is on silent.

Download the app
  1. Tap Download the app, then open the downloaded file.
  2. If Android asks, allow installing from this source.
  3. Tap Install, open FamilyPing, and type the family code.
